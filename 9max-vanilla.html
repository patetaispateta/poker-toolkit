vs BTN 3-bet (you're OOP): 4-bet AA/KK/QQ/AKs/AKo. Call TT–88, AQs–AJs, KQs, JTs, T9s. Fold most else including ATo, KQo.

BTN open vs 3-bet — you always have position (except vs BB)
vs SB 3-bet: You're IP. Call very wide — TT–22, all broadway suited, ATs–A2s, KQs–K9s, all suited connectors down to 54s, AJo+, KQo. 4-bet AA/KK/QQ/AKs/AKo + A5s/A4s bluffs.
vs BB 3-bet: Still IP. Similar wide call range. BB defends wide so their 3-bet range is strong — tighten slightly. TT–33, AJs+, KQs–KTs, QJs–QTs, JTs–54s suited, AKo–AJo.
4-bet value both cases: AA, KK, QQ, JJ, AKo/s. 4-bet bluffs: A5s, A4s, sometimes A3s.
Key mindset: From the BTN you're almost always IP vs a 3-bet. Exploit that. Fold EV is low — calling with position and playable hands is almost always better than folding too much.
SB open vs 3-bet — you're always OOP
This is your toughest spot. You opened wide and now face a 3-bet OOP with only the BB behind.
4-bet value: AA, KK, QQ, AKs, AKo. These are clear. Don't slow play AA/KK here.
4-bet bluffs: A5s, A4s, occasionally suited connectors with blockers (e.g. A3s). Keep your 4-bet range polarised.
Call range (very tight): JJ, TT, 99, AQs, AJs, KQs, QJs, JTs. That's roughly it vs a solid 3-bettor.
Fold the rest: Including AQo, KQo, ATo, 88–22. They look playable but lose too much EV OOP with no position to realise equity.
Lesson: This is why SB opens need to be disciplined — your defence range is brutally limited when 3-bet.
Key Reminders — Read Before You Play
Quick mental checklist to keep you disciplined during sessions.
Before the session
Check your mood

If you're tilted, tired, or emotional — do not play. Log off. Come back tomorrow.

Set your stop-loss

Decide your max spend before opening a single table. Stick to it no matter what.

Stake check

BR above $800? $5 games as primary. $10 as occasional shot. BR below $400? Drop to $2s immediately.

During play
Cooler vs mistake?

Before you tilt — ask: was that hand a mathematical cooler or a mistake I made? Only fix mistakes.

Tighten if unsure

If you're in a marginal spot and unsure — default to fold. Especially in early position.

Position awareness

Before every open, ask: how many players are left to act? Widen as position improves.

3-bet = slow down

Facing a 3-bet? Stop. Breathe. Check this guide. Avoid snapping in marginal hands out of ego.

Stack depth matters

Pairs and suited connectors need deep stacks to setmine. Tighten these at 30BB or less.

After a bad beat
5-minute rule

Take a 5 minute break after any big hand — win or loss. Don't make the next decision on adrenaline.

One tournament at a time

Don't fire into a new tournament to "get it back." Revenge entries = spew.

Log it and move on

Add the hand to your tracker notes. One line is enough. Process it after the session, not during.

Key Rules
BB vs SB is the widest defence spot in poker. You're IP, SB opens wide, pot odds are favourable — defend nearly everything.
3-bet to build pots with strong hands. Don't just flat JJ/QQ — 3-bet and build the pot. SB will call wide.
Raise frequency from BB is high. Mix plenty of 3-bets into your BB defence — SB's wide range folds often enough to make bluff 3-bets very profitable.
