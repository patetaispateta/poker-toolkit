BTN 6-max (~55%+ range, 30BB+)
Open nearly 100% vs two blinds. Position is king in 6-max hypers.
Only fold: The very weakest off-suit trash when facing tight blinds (72o, 62o, 52o, 32o type hands).
Sizing: Min-raise (2x) from BTN is standard in hypers — cheap steal, preserves stack.
Adapt: Tighten vs known 3-bet happy blinds. Loosen vs passive/tight players.
SB 6-max — open vs BB only (30BB+)
Open range ~55%: All pairs, all Ax, all Kx suited, all Qx/Jx/Tx suited, all offsuit broadways, most offsuit Kx/Qx down to K7o, Q8o
Never limp SB: Raise or fold. Limping SB in 6-max is heavily exploitable.
Sizing: 3x to make BB's call uncomfortable. Min-raise is exploitable in SB vs BB.
Note: BB will defend wide so your post-flop skills matter — don't over-widen with hands you can't play well OOP.
15–30BB mid-stack: Postflop play shrinks drastically. Shift toward a raise-or-fold strategy. Pairs and suited connectors lose setmine value — open them only if you're willing to call a 3-bet shove. Suited Ax becomes premium (fold equity + nut flush draw). All opens should be 2.2x or min-raise.
Mid-stack RFI adjustments (15–30BB) — all positions
UTG/HJ: Tighten to ~AA–77, AKo–ATo, KQo–KJo, AKs–A9s, KQs–KTs, QJs–JTs, T9s, 98s. Fold speculative hands — you can't see cheap flops.
CO: Widen slightly. Add AJs–A7s, Q9s–J9s, 88–66, suited connectors 87s–65s. Fold weak offsuit hands.
BTN: Open around 40–50% but be prepared to fold or shove vs 3-bet — no more calling in position with speculative hands at this depth.
SB: Raise or fold. Range ~40%. At 20BB your opens invite shoves — tighten to hands you're happy getting it in with.
Key rule: If you open and get 3-bet, you need a clear call/fold/4-bet plan. No "I'll see a flop" at 20BB.
Under 15BB — push/fold territory. Traditional raises no longer make sense. Move to the Push/Fold tab for complete shove ranges by position and stack depth.
Short-stack strategy overview (<15BB)
10–15BB: Open-shove or fold from all positions. Min-raises are exploitable and commit too much. See Push/Fold tab.
6–10BB: Shove very wide from BTN/SB (~70%+). From UTG/HJ still tight (AA–TT, AKo/s, AQs).
Under 6BB: Any two cards from BTN/SB vs a fold. Call off vs any raise with Ax, Kx, pairs, two broadway cards.
Never min-raise short: A min-raise at 10BB commits 20% of your stack and lets opponents shove profitably over you.
vs 3-Bet Defence 6-Max Hyper
6-max players 3-bet far more frequently than full-ring. Adjust your defence accordingly — fold equity goes up, calling range goes down vs aggressive 3-bettors.
Key hyper difference: In hypers stacks are shallower faster. At 25–30BB a 3-bet to ~7–8BB effectively forces a call/fold/4-bet-shove decision — there's rarely a "call and play postflop" option. Polarise your 4-bets and fold more non-premiums than you would in a deepstack game.

CO open vs 3-bet
vs BTN 3-bet (OOP): 4-bet AA/KK/QQ/AKs/AKo/AQs. 4-bet bluff A5s/A4s. Call TT–88, AQo, AJs, KQs, JTs — very little else.
vs SB/BB 3-bet (IP): Wider call range. Add 77–55, ATs–A9s, KJs–KTs, QJs–QTs, T9s–87s–76s–65s. Still fold weak offsuit like KQo when sizing is large.
Stack depth check: At 20BB a CO open to 2.2x + 3-bet to ~6.5BB = you have ~13BB behind. Calling postflop is impossible — just 4-bet shove your entire continuing range or fold.
BTN open vs 3-bet (SB or BB)
Always IP. Best spot to defend wide in 6-max.
4-bet value: AA, KK, QQ, JJ, AKo/s, AQs. Shove if <25BB effective.
4-bet bluffs: A5s, A4s, A3s, occasionally K5s–K4s suited (blocker + suited equity).
Call range vs BB: TT–22, AJs–A2s, KQs–K9s, QJs–Q9s, JTs–54s, AQo–ATo, KQo–KJo.
Call range vs SB: SB 3-bet range is tighter than BB — tighten your call slightly. Drop 22–33, weakest suited connectors.
Hyper caveat: At <25BB even BTN calls require stack-depth awareness. Calling TT at 18BB vs a 3-bet then folding to a flop shove is a massive mistake — plan for getting it in.
SB open vs BB 3-bet — toughest spot in 6-max hypers
You're OOP for the rest of the hand. Defence range is brutally narrow.
4-bet value: AA, KK, QQ, AKs, AKo. Non-negotiable shoves at most hyper stack depths.
4-bet bluffs: A5s, A4s only. Resist the urge to add more — BB is in position on you the whole hand.
Call (rarely): JJ, TT, AQs only — and only if deep enough to have postflop value (>30BB).
Fold the rest: AQo, AJs, 99–22, all suited connectors. Brutal but correct. This is why SB opens should be disciplined in hypers.
Hyper lesson: Opening wide from SB in hypers is fine — but have a plan for when you get 3-bet. Most of your range simply folds.

Hyper-Specific Reminders 6-Max Hyper
Things that catch players out specifically in 6-max hyper formats.
Format differences to internalise
Blinds escalate fast

In hypers you go from 30BB to 15BB in a few orbits. Know your BB count at all times. Recalculate every level.

6-max = 3-bet happy

Expect 3-bets far more often than full-ring. Don't widen calling range just because you feel 3-bet light — fold more non-premiums OOP.

ICM hits earlier

Pay jumps come quickly. Near the bubble, tighten calling shoves and widen shoving — fold equity is massive.

No setmining under 20BB

Calling opens with 22–55 hoping to flop a set does not work below 20BB. Not enough implied odds. Just fold or shove.

Common 6-max hyper mistakes
Over-defending vs 3-bets

6-max players 3-bet light. But calling OOP with ATo, KQo, 88 at 20BB is still a losing play — the stack depth kills your postflop edge.

Min-raising short

At 10–12BB a min-raise commits 20% of your stack and lets opponents shove profitably. Shove or fold. There is no middle ground.

Waiting for a hand

At 6BB the blinds eat you alive if you wait. Shove any playable hand. The EV of folding and waiting is negative.

Fancy play syndrome

Hypers are not the format for trapping, slow-plays, or multi-street bluffs. Play straightforward and aggressive. Fancy costs chips.

Mood & tilt check
Variance is extreme

Hypers have higher variance than regular MTTs. Losing 10 in a row is completely normal. Do not adjust strategy due to results.

Fire-and-forget mindset

You will bust many tournaments in 3 hands. That is the format. Register, play well, move on. Do not chase losses in a single session.

Session stop-loss

Set a max spend before playing. Hypers burn through BR fast — 10 x $5 = $50 in under an hour if you multi-table.
